## Explanation revision

Proposed Dutch examples illustrate the requested change; they are **not yet generated, approved or educationally validated**:

- Zero: “Er komen geen bij. Daarom blijft 9 + 0 gelijk aan 9.” For `0 + 9`, explain starting with nothing and adding nine; handle `0 + 0` explicitly.
- Crossing ten, `7 + 9`: “Maak eerst 10. Splits 9 in 3 en 6. 7 + 3 = 10. Daarna 10 + 6 = 16.”
- Doubles, `6 + 6`: “5 + 5 = 10. Bij allebei komt er 1 bij: 10 + 2 = 12.”
- Other small sums: count on from the larger number with explicit useful steps, rather than restating the key.

Define deterministic strategy precedence for overlapping cases (zero, doubles, crossing ten, other), and verify every intermediate value, remainder and final answer across all 100 pairs with an independent oracle. Version explanation templates and inventory together. Preserve the old review package; changed text requires a new package with visible differences. Technical verification covers every variant. Human review should cover each distinct strategy and its boundaries, not all 100 sums or unchanged screens. These templates do not create independent assessment families merely by changing wording.
