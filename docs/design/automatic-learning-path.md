## Revision 2 · evaluation fixes, 19 October 2026

The user authorized fixing the audit findings and agent-owned retesting while keeping the prototype synthetic and in memory. **In Review**, not approved. The earlier revision-1 audit below remains historical evidence; its three engineering failures are addressed by this revision. Educational assessment accuracy is still unvalidated.

- **Answer-position leakage:** vocabulary options and listed ordering numbers are deterministically shuffled when a task is issued. Position no longer follows the vocabulary prompt wording. The stored question array is reused for drafts, navigation, carryover and identical retries; nothing reshuffles during an attempt. The fixed review seed makes tests reproducible, not unpredictable or secure. Guessing, memorizing fixture answers and other test-taking strategies remain possible; this does not establish a false-positive rate or make two-choice questions sufficient for mastery.
- **Numeric equivalence:** the route adapter accepts the same valid whole-number representations as the approved numerical exercise (including leading zeros). It first enforces the existing one-to-six-digit input shape, then compares numerical values. It retains submitted text such as `029` in feedback/history. Blank, malformed, decimal, exponent, hex and overlength strings do not become correct through permissive number conversion. Taal's finite-word case/whitespace policy is unchanged; no spelling correction or fuzzy semantic grading was added.
- **Fresh-check inventory:** each category has disjoint semantic item pools for assessment versus practice/repair. Taal fixtures now contain 24 entries per objective: 12 reserved for checks and 12 for practice. Numerical pools contain 36 source items per objective in each partition. Practice may reuse earlier practice items across tasks, but its ten originals remain distinct within each task. Checks continue excluding all previously issued item identities. Practice cannot consume confirmation/retention inventory. Truly exhausted check inventory still produces an explicit unavailable-content notice, never a fabricated pass or recycled “fresh” question.
- **Narrow arithmetic examples:** addition now varies both operands and the units digits, with separate no-unit-carry/unit-carry examples and arithmetically checked explanations. It remains a small whole-number blueprint, not comprehensive coverage of addition. The remaining place-value, language and word-problem templates are still deliberately narrow.
- **Evidence wording:** guardian copy describes first answers on new questions and explicitly says different question forms do not prove independent mastery. The demonstration gate still uses two scoped goals and two forms; this revision does not silently approve or recalibrate that rule.

## Explicit demonstration policy

`synthetic-path-v2` is an unvalidated, deterministic review rule, exposed in the guardian view. It is not a calibrated ability estimator, school level or validated threshold.

- Each supported category has exactly two named, bounded objectives. The entry check samples each with two distinct item identities and two question forms. All must be correct on the first attempt in a completed check to proceed directly to fresh confirmation. A high average cannot hide a missing objective.
- A clean entry check skips the unassigned ten-question drills. It does not immediately declare mastery. A separate four-question confirmation with unexposed items covers both objectives before automatic advancement, at the next daily assignment boundary.
- A gap receives ten practice questions focused on the uncovered objective(s), using the existing feedback and second-miss explanation. Practice completion schedules a fresh whole-category confirmation, regardless of the weighted task score. Supported success, repeated/exposed items and retry success are excluded from independent advancement evidence.
- Incomplete checks never pass. The insufficient-evidence history contains one actual submitted answer and resumes at its next question. The other histories contain actual completed attempt records, including successful deferred retries for the gap history; they are not outcome-only flags.
- After two unsuccessful confirmation tasks, that main route stops for appropriate explanation/support. It does not loop indefinitely, jump categories, or diagnose a learning problem. Other subjects and daily Redactiesommen continue. Resuming a support pause is outside this read-only prototype.
- Retention becomes due two preview dates after confirmation, then three dates after a successful retention check. One fresh item per objective gives only a review signal. A miss schedules bounded, objective-specific repair followed by another fresh check. Two failed retention checks stop that maintenance loop for support without erasing completed categories or moving the main route backwards. The oldest due item gets the single maintenance slot; others wait.
- Limited fixture inventory is explicit. Independent checks must have unused semantic item identities; if eligible items run out, no pass or unrelated substitute is fabricated. Guardian notices explain the gap. This does not implement validated item-family independence: forms, content and cutoffs still need educational review.

The histories are **Instapstof al bekend**, **Eén gericht oefenpunt**, and **Nog onvoldoende bewijs**. Their names describe fictional setup, not observations about Adam. Daily word-problem outcomes remain task results and do not automatically grant reading or operation mastery.
