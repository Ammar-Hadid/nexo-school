# Nexai: a consistent 2.5D animation method

18 October 2026. Research and recommendation only; no new artwork, rig, dependency or runtime change. Official documentation was checked on this date. Existing Nexai research lives beside its design references; this cross-revision assessment uses the explicitly requested `docs/research/` location.

## Finding

**Stop generating additional independent sprite drawings as the continuity fix.** For Nexai's seated, expressive movements, the recommended next method is a carefully prepared layered puppet: reuse the approved artwork, separate the parts that actually need movement, and animate those stable parts with bones, limited mesh deformation and authored timing. This is a recommendation from the evidence below, not a claim that a successful rig already exists or that software alone guarantees quality.

## Local evidence

- The [character bible](../design/nexai-character-bible.md) and [frozen manifest](../design/references/nexai/master-v1/manifest.json) approve master v1 and its greeting. They explicitly say the head, ears and tail are not independent layers, and there is no approved side/back turnaround. The current asset is therefore not a complete animation puppet.
- Inspection of [`NexaiArtwork`](../../src/components/nexai/nexai-artwork.tsx) shows playback changing a sprite's source, crop and silhouette at exposure boundaries. It does not deform a stable character mesh. The [previous research](../design/references/nexai/motion-v2/research.md) already acknowledges inconsistent generated anatomy and excluded drawings. Additional exposures can reduce a temporal gap, but cannot by themselves correct changed identity. This last conclusion is an inference from the implementation and documented source problems.
- The existing [motion tests](../../src/components/nexai/nexai-motion.test.ts) check timing, registration and related behavior. Such checks cannot establish that two drawings depict the same face or that an action looks natural. These tests were inspected, not rerun for this documentation task.

## What the primary sources establish

| Mechanism                | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Consequence for Nexai                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Layer preparation        | Live2D describes separating an illustration into movable parts and drawing areas that become visible when those parts move, on additional editable layers. [Material separation](https://docs.live2d.com/en/cubism-editor-manual/divide-the-material/)                                                                                              | Cutting out an ear or arm is insufficient when its movement exposes missing fur underneath. That fill requires deliberate artwork preparation.                   |
| Mesh deformation         | Spine defines a mesh as an image-textured polygon whose vertices can be moved. Bone weights drive those vertices; topology affects the resulting deformation. [Meshes](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-meshes), [weights](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-weights)                                                                            | One consistent texture can bend through motion. A mesh is not a source of new anatomy or unseen painted surfaces.                                                |
| Timing and interpolation | Spine supports held, linear and Bézier interpolation of keyed values. Its breakdown poses describe how parts move between the main poses. [Graph editor](https://esotericsoftware.com/spine-graph)                                                                                                                                                  | Author the paw's arc and the body's response, then interpolate those parameters. Raising the playback callback rate does not add meaningful in-between drawings. |
| Rig limitations          | Adobe documents overlap controls for apparent depth and warns that inadequate position/rotation animation can shear or stretch artwork. Bend pins support effects such as breathing and tail movement. [Puppet tools](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/animate-in-after-effects/animate-with-puppet-tools/animating-puppet-tools.html) | A rig still needs restrained deformation, depth decisions and visual cleanup. More pins or bones do not automatically produce convincing movement.               |

## Recommended production path — inference

1. Preserve master v1 unchanged. Prepare a separate, editable candidate puppet with only the layers needed for the first action, retaining the approved visible pixels wherever possible. Have an illustrator/rigging artist finish revealed areas and joints where extraction alone cannot work. A paws-down neutral pose is additional artwork and needs its own review.
2. Prove one short seated action before expanding the catalogue: neutral rest, deliberate paw lift, greeting, and return. Use explicit pivots, modest weighted bending, protected facial proportions, purposeful easing and small delayed secondary movement. Review the assembled neutral pose and the action beside the approved master at equal scale.
3. Evaluate a dedicated authoring tool against that proof. Spine's documented image meshes, weights and curves make it a concrete candidate; Live2D demonstrates the same underlying need for prepared parts. This report does not select, purchase or integrate either product. In particular, Spine's documentation says mesh attachments are unavailable in Essential; edition and runtime requirements need evaluation before adoption.
4. Retain pause, reduced motion, offscreen suspension and full-body bounds. Test the resulting asset at its actual display size and on the target tablet before promising smoothness or choosing a delivery format.

## Turns and rolling onto the back

A modest head turn can sometimes be suggested through layered movement and controlled deformation. **A convincing roll that reveals Nexai's back, belly or underside cannot be derived faithfully from the current front-view pixels alone.** This is a geometric/artwork inference from the mesh mechanisms and the missing-view inventory, rather than a vendor claim that 2D rolling is impossible.

There are two credible routes for those larger actions: deliberately authored and reviewed additional views/transition drawings, combined with the puppet where useful; or a separately approved 3D reconstruction. Consistent hand-authored cel animation is also valid—the problem is treating separately generated images as guaranteed in-betweens. A 2D rig can stage a roll using purpose-made assets, but cannot invent its hidden surfaces. The rejected Blender work should not be restored merely to bypass this decision.

The immediate recommendation is a small artist-prepared rig proof, not another generation batch or a promise to fix all twelve clips. Professional appearance still depends on art preparation, posing and motion judgment. No new artwork or 3D change was performed as part of this research.
