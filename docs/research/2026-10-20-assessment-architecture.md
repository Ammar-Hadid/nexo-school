## Research and its limits

Sources accessed 20 October 2026; source claims and our choices are separated below.

- [SLO formative evaluation](https://www.slo.nl/thema/meer/10-14-onderwijs/werken-leerdoelen/slag-formatief-evalueren/) describes clarifying goals, eliciting observations, interpretation, communication and adaptation; uncertainty calls for further information. **Our inference:** select an unmet objective/facet and disclose coverage instead of averaging across skills. This source concerns a general formative cycle, not a validated automated groep-5 test.
- [SLO learning progressions](https://www.slo.nl/thema/vakspecifieke-thema/rekenen-wiskunde/leerlijnen/) describes reasoned sequences and distinctions between concept formation, procedures, representations and application. **Our inference:** a category blueprint must define required competencies and prerequisites. The two-objective demo is not a complete SLO-aligned category or validated groep-5 curriculum.
- [EEF mastery learning](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/mastery-learning) supports diagnostics, sequencing and additional support with a high success standard, while rating the evidence as limited and noting implementation variation. Reported conventional thresholds concern particular tests and interventions. **Our decision:** no generic 80–90% skip rule and no claim that EEF validates individualized automatic assignment.
- [NRC, Knowing What Students Know, chapter 2](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10019/chapter/4) links cognition, observations and interpretation. Assessment infers capabilities from observations. **Our decision:** a correct scored answer becomes evidence; the category decision is a separate, replaceable interpretation. Even diverse correct answers do not directly reveal reasoning.
- [Carpenter, Pan and Butler (2022)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00089-1) reviews spacing and retrieval across learning contexts. The publisher abstract and reference information were accessible; the full review was subscription restricted. **Our inference:** immediate success and delayed retrieval should be distinguished. This does not establish Nexo's two-/three-day demo intervals.
- [IES elementary mathematics intervention guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/26) recommends systematic instruction, mathematical language, representations, number lines and word-problem instruction. **Our inference:** numerical procedures alone cannot stand in for application. A typed numerical decomposition is still only a constrained conceptual proxy, not an explanation of the child's reasoning.

## Alternatives considered before implementation

| Approach                                   | Requirements and uncertainty                                                                                                                         | Explainability / false-advancement risk                                                                                       | Decision                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Explicit per-skill evidence rules          | Authored blueprint, item metadata and coherent records; counts and exclusions are observable, thresholds remain uncalibrated                         | Auditable missing facets and conflicts; wrong blueprints or weak content can still yield false readiness                      | Implement first, with provisional labels and fail-closed missing content |
| Bayesian Knowledge Tracing                 | Skill mapping, initial knowledge, learning, guess and slip parameters; fits need representative longitudinal data                                    | Latent-state probabilities are model-dependent; correlated templates and parameter misspecification can inflate estimates     | Preserve replaceable projection boundary; no invented probabilities      |
| IRT / calibrated item models               | Item calibration and model-fit evidence, appropriate dimensionality and treatment of dependencies; adaptive selection needs a usable calibrated pool | Item information can improve selection, but precision does not establish adequate breadth or correct construct interpretation | Defer until enough suitable lawful data and reviewed items exist         |
| Evidence-centered design / educator rubric | Explicit claim, task requirements and interpretation argument; independent content review and outcome validation                                     | Helps establish what a task can support; complements rules or statistical models rather than providing a magic score          | Use now as blueprint/review methodology                                  |

Primary technical sources: [Corbett and Anderson, original knowledge-tracing paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01099821) models changing procedural knowledge in a programming tutor; that domain does not validate a Dutch primary-school transfer. [ETS, Basic Concepts of IRT](https://www.ets.org/Media/Research/pdf/RM-20-06.pdf) explains item calibration and statistical assumptions; [ETS small-sample calibration study](https://www.ets.org/research/policy_research_reports/publications/report/2024/kgvw.html) illustrates calibration difficulties with small samples. [ETS evidence-centered assessment design](https://www.ets.org/research/policy_research_reports/publications/report/2018/jzuw.html) provides an example of explicitly connecting constructs, tasks and interpretation. The original knowledge-tracing publisher page exposed the abstract, not the full paper; the authors’ [pyBKT implementation documentation](https://github.com/CAHLR/pyBKT) independently describes prior, learning, guess and slip parameters. No BKT package was installed. These sources motivate architectural boundaries; none validates this policy's numbers.

## Real-child validation milestone

The user activated the [preparation brief](../../tasks/assessment-validation-handoff.md) on 20 October. The [bounded blueprint and review package](assessment-validation/blueprint.md), [independent-educator protocol](assessment-validation/educator-protocol.md) and [executed synthetic evidence](assessment-validation/executed-report.md) are prepared. The fixed policy and interactive route are unchanged; content is Draft/synthetic and preparation tooling In Review. The real-child validation below remains a separately authorized milestone requiring actual independent educational review.

Before decisions for Adam, an independent Dutch primary educator should define the precise objectives, prerequisite scope, difficulty and acceptable responses for one bounded category. Review keys, ambiguity, representation accessibility and family independence. Construct a held-out bank with different authored families and, for reading, different passages; keep it out of selection, practice and threshold tuning.

Run shadow recommendations with adult oversight and separately scheduled held-out confirmation, including delayed tasks. Pre-register how disagreement is adjudicated by an educator who has not seen the engine's label. Record false advancement, missed foundational weaknesses, unnecessary questions/practice, abstention/content coverage, time burden and later reversals, broken down by skill, response type, assistance and accessibility needs. Determine acceptable false-advancement risk, uncertainty bounds, sample requirements and stopping rules with that reviewer before promoting a policy. Zero observed errors in a small sample is insufficient. A single learner cannot calibrate a general system.

Disagreement should trigger content or policy investigation and reversible support, not an automatic diagnosis of the child. Corrected keys/evaluations append new revisions and rebuild projections. Guardian overrides remain independent decisions, never mastery observations. Production privacy/authentication, persistence, consent, content publication and operating safeguards still need their separate authorized milestones.
