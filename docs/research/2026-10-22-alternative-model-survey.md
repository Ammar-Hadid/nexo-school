# Alternative model providers for D13

22 October 2026. Research only; no live inference, spending, new provider integration, educational validation or publication authorization. This is a supporting survey for the cross-provider D13 recommendation, not an exhaustive register of every historical checkpoint. Read alongside the [D13 handoff](../engineering/review-benchmark.md). Current ledger, approved roadmap, development protocol and benchmark contract were inspected; existing working-tree changes were preserved.

## Findings

No source examined establishes that a model reliably creates or reviews Dutch primary-school material. Language support, reasoning benchmarks and valid JSON are candidate-selection signals, not educational evidence. The recommendations below are inferences to test on the same independently adjudicated material. D11 arithmetic should remain deterministic.

Prices are standard USD per million text input/output tokens, excluding taxes and exchange-rate conversion, unless stated otherwise. They are research observations, not configured D12 reservation bounds. Reasoning output, retries and unknown usage must also fit the eventual cap. Region, deployment scope, model version and current billable terms must be pinned before execution.

## Evidence and caveats by provider

**Mistral.** The [model catalogue](https://docs.mistral.ai/models) and [pricing table](https://docs.mistral.ai/inference/pricing) support the lineup and rates above. [Small 4's comparison card](https://docs.mistral.ai/getting-started/models/compare?models=mistral-small-4-0-26-03) identifies `mistral-small-2603`; the [language documentation](https://docs.mistral.ai/resources/languages) explicitly includes Dutch. The [Large 4 model card](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4) dates its public preview to 6 October 2026, supports structured outputs and batch, and shows a temporary 50% sale; use the undiscounted standard rate for planning unless the applicable promotion is frozen. Small 4 is the recommended alternative entrant because it combines low token cost, explicit Dutch coverage and a suitable output interface. That is a testable hypothesis, not a quality finding.

**DeepSeek.** [Current models and pricing](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/) identify V4.1 Flash-1017 and V4 Pro-0813, JSON output and thinking/non-thinking modes. Retired V4 Flash aliases silently route to V4.1 Flash. Pin the observed served version and explicit thinking mode; a working old model name does not prove unchanged behavior. Dutch-specific evaluation was not found in the inspected primary pages. JSON output alone must not be interpreted as schema or semantic correctness.

**Qwen.** [3.8 Max](https://docs.modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/en/model-studio/qwen3-8-max) and [3.8 Flash](https://docs.modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/en/model-studio/qwen3-8-flash) show both international and global rates; global prices are lower ($1.65/$4.951 and $0.113/$0.382 respectively), but they must not be substituted for an international deployment. [API documentation](https://docs.modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/en/model-studio/anthropic-api-messages) documents strict schema output for 3.8/3.7. The Flash card and [batch guide](https://docs.modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/en/model-studio/batch-inference) disagree on batch availability in extracted content; confirm actual region/model support rather than budget on a presumed discount. No Dutch educational evidence was established. Latest Flash is the preferred Qwen candidate, not a stale Qwen3 snapshot selected from memory.

**Z.ai.** [Official pricing](https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/pricing) supports the table. Full GLM-5.3 is also [hosted by Mistral](https://docs.mistral.ai/models), allowing a provider/deployment choice distinct from the model creator. Structured-output guarantees are endpoint-specific; they were not verified for every direct Z.ai mode here. Do not infer Dutch reliability from a low price or coding claims.

**Kimi.** [Current model list](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/models) lists K3, K2.6 and K2.7 Code variants, and says K2.5 and Moonshot-v1 retired on 31 August 2026; K2 retired in May. [K3 documentation](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/guide/kimi-k3-quickstart) is current. The [pricing page](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/pricing/chat) confirms pay-as-you-go billing and separate K3 cache writes but its numerical tables were not extractable. No price invented; verify before shortlisting a paid run. JSON-mode documentation is linked from the model list; exact K3 schema semantics remain to inspect.

**Cohere.** The [A+ card](https://docs.cohere.com/v1/docs/command-a-plus) explicitly covers all EU languages and structured outputs, and describes free rate-limited API access plus Model Vault production. [Command A](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/command-a) provides its token rates. [Vault pricing](https://docs.cohere.com/docs/model-vault/standard/pricing) lists A+ at $17.50/hour Fixed or $32.50/hour Flex and describes minimum capacity/commitments. That deployment is unsuitable for this project's €10 monthly envelope; a constrained free experiment does not resolve production hosting. Older/Aya variants are not automatically better because they emphasize multilingual use.

**Amazon.** [Nova 2 documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/nova/latest/nova2-userguide/) distinguishes text-producing Nova 2 Lite from Sonic and embeddings. Its [responsible-AI service card](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/pdfs/ai/responsible-ai/nova-2-lite/nova-2-lite.pdf) lists Dutch among 15 optimized languages. An [AWS worked pricing example](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/pair-nova-2-lite-with-claude-for-cost-optimized-document-processing/) gives the cited rates; recheck account/region pricing before a paid run. Nova 2 Sonic's language list excludes Dutch, and audio is outside this text benchmark. Exact structured-output support for the intended Bedrock invocation remains to verify.

**xAI.** [Grok 4.7's current card](https://docs.x.ai/developers/models/grok-4.7) documents structured outputs, the cited rates and reasoning levels; default reasoning is high, so set it explicitly. [Grok 4.3](https://docs.x.ai/developers/models/grok-4.3) provides the lower comparison rate. [Retirement documentation](https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/may-15-retirement) states that older Grok 4.1/4 fast names redirect to 4.3 at its higher prices. Treat catalogue/search freshness cautiously: older pricing snapshots still foreground 4.6 while the current card identifies 4.7.

**Meta and open-model hosts.** [Meta's model card](https://github.com/meta-llama/llama-models/blob/main/models/llama4/MODEL_CARD.md) lists 12 supported languages, without Dutch. [Meta's developer page](https://ai.meta.com/llama/get-started/) advertises Muse Spark but does not establish accessible API economics in this survey. Hosting through Together, Fireworks, Groq, Cerebras, Hugging Face, OpenRouter or a hyperscaler changes rates, schema support, routing and data handling; those are inference providers rather than independent model-quality candidates. Self-hosting a large model would add infrastructure and operating cost and is not justified by this application’s tiny budget. This note does not authorize it.

## Narrowing for D13

The primary recommendation is to add **Mistral Small 4** as a low-cost alternative alongside the main cross-provider contenders chosen in the parent research. If one additional low-cost entrant is affordable to integrate, use **Qwen3.8 Flash** or **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash**, rather than spending the initial evaluation budget testing every historical checkpoint. The alternatives are not rejected as inferior; they are deferred to keep the comparison useful and implementable. GLM-5.3-Flash is a credible reserve on price; Nova 2 Lite on explicit Dutch support; Cohere A+ on multilingual evidence but not current production economics. Grok 4.7, Kimi K3 and Mistral Large 4 preview remain optional stronger challengers.

First screen candidates on the same human-adjudicated development material, then compare survivors on genuinely unused, family/passage-separated holdout material. Keep generation quality separate from reviewer quality: a good writer may miss its own mistakes. Include correct-but-unusual Dutch answers, ambiguous distractors, grammar/spelling errors, missing reading evidence and mismatched learning objectives. Report critical misses, false alarms, abstentions, malformed output, reviewer time, all-attempt cost and cost per accepted exercise. Cross-provider reviewing may diversify mistakes but does not make judgments independently correct.

## Additional coverage check

**MiniMax deserves explicit consideration.** Its current [model catalogue](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/models-intro) lists **MiniMax-M3.1-Flash** and **MiniMax-M3**, while older search/subscription pages still foreground M2.7. The current [pay-as-you-go table](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/pricing/overview) lists M3.1-Flash at **$0.15/$0.60** per million input/output tokens. M3 at up to 512K input is billed **$0.30/$1.20**, described as a permanent 50% reduction from $0.60/$2.40; longer input and priority processing cost more. These are credible economical reserve candidates. The inspected descriptions emphasize coding and agentic work; no Dutch educational advantage or exact response-schema guarantee was established. If expanding the initial shortlist, compare M3.1-Flash alongside Qwen3.8 Flash and DeepSeek Flash in a small screening round, then advance only the best measured candidates. No need to purchase a coding subscription for API benchmarking.

**AI21.** The official [Jamba page](https://www.ai21.com/jamba/) currently promotes **Jamba2 Mini**, **Jamba2 3B** and **Jamba Reasoning 3B**, with self-hosted and cloud-partner deployment. The current public [pricing page](https://www.ai21.com/pricing/) did not expose a usable numerical per-token table in retrieval, and a former documentation URL returned 404. Do not quote old Jamba 1.5/1.6 rates as current Jamba2 rates. Dutch-specific evidence and a suitable simple paid endpoint remain unverified. Defer the initial comparison because no demonstrated task-specific advantage offsets another integration; this is not a finding of inferior quality.

**IBM Granite.** IBM's current official model collection contains **Granite 4.2 at 3B, 8B and 30B**. The [30B model card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.2-30b) and [3B card](https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-4.2-3b) explicitly list Dutch among tested languages and offer reasoning modes under Apache 2.0. These are credible later local/deployment candidates. Open weights do not specify a universal hosted API price; exact deployment cost and schema behavior remain unverified. The initial D13 benchmark does not justify adding an inference server or GPU to the existing VPS.

**Dutch/European specialists.** The [EuroLLM project](https://eurollm.io/) now identifies **EuroLLM-22B Instruct** as its flagship, alongside 9B and 1.7B, with all 24 EU languages supported and 32K context for 22B. This makes 22B a useful specialist control if a suitable hosted endpoint becomes available, but does not establish superiority on grammar, age suitability or defect detection. [GPT-NL's project owner](https://www.tno.nl/en/digital/artificial-intelligence/gpt-nl/) describes a Dutch model with controlled licensing; no self-service per-token offer suitable for this benchmark was verified. **GEITje 7B/Ultra** is historically relevant, but the original author [withdrew his weights on 25 January 2025](https://goingdutch.ai/nl/posts/geitje-takedown/). Do not use old availability claims to nominate it as an immediately deployable supported service. Dutch specialization is a reason to evaluate a model, not to bypass the same independent reference-set requirements.

## Verification and omissions

Primary-source web research and local contract inspection completed. No benchmark/model-quality experiment, API request, code test, paid operation, real learner data, remote change or runtime edit was performed. This note changes no acceptance status. Exact region/account access, privacy terms, response-schema support and billable bounds for the final selected candidates remain pre-run work. The coordinating chat owns the ledger amendment.
